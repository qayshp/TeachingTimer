# Teaching Timer support

Email [teachingtimer@qays.net](mailto:teachingtimer@qays.net) for private help or
to report a problem. A short description is enough to start; no logs are required.

Useful details, when convenient:

- App version/build from **Settings → About**, and Watch/watchOS version. Open
  Settings from **Class** on public beta build 37 or the gear on newer builds.
- What you were doing, the approximate time and time zone, and what helped.
- For media issues: phone or Watch playback, the audio output, and whether
  sound, volume, controls or song progress stopped working.

Do not interrupt a class to gather evidence. You do not need to include student
information or other personal details. Never send passwords or verification codes.

## Sending from your phone or computer

Scan the support QR code on the Watch with your phone. On public beta build 37,
use **Class → Settings → About → Contact support**. On newer builds, use
**gear → Settings → Help → Contact support**. You can also email the address
above directly or use **Contact support** in the optional iPhone app. The QR
code opens this guide. Attachments are optional.

## Optional iPhone diagnostics companion

If your installed build includes the companion, open Teaching Timer on both
paired devices. On iPhone, choose **Last 2 hours**, **Last 24 hours**, or **All
retained events**, then **Get diagnostics from Watch**. Alternatively, use
Watch **Settings → Help → Send diagnostics to iPhone** to prepare, preview and
send a report. Teaching works without the phone app.

On iPhone, check the saved report's preparation time and selected window, then
choose **Email support**. Apple Mail supplies our address and the attachment;
review the message and send it. If Mail is not configured, **Report options → Export received
report…** offers other apps, where you may need to enter the support address.
Nothing is emailed automatically. Delete the received report when finished.

If a request fails, compare the phone's build at the top with Watch **Settings →
About**. **Connection details** helps distinguish installation, reachability,
queueing and receipt. Keep the Watch app open for a phone request. A delayed
report can still arrive after the wait expires; check its preparation time.

The companion is absent from build 37. See [build availability](DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md)
and [recorded companion tests](docs/OPTIONAL_COMPANION.md).

## Public reports and known issues

You can also use the [GitHub issue form](https://github.com/qayshp/TeachingTimer/issues/new/choose)
for a sanitized public description; GitHub sign-in is required. Do not post raw
journals or system logs there. Review screenshots for private information first.
The [known-issue guide](docs/ISSUE_TRACKER.md) lists current problems and mitigations.

See the [privacy policy](docs/PRIVACY.md) for local data and voluntary sharing.
